# Film 02 — “Your Padova. Your space.” Concept

**Audience:** UniPD students, incoming Erasmus students, and international
students looking for a furnished place in Padova.
**Format:** 9:16 Reel, 1080×1920, 60 fps. The length follows the voice-over
(about 32 s with the scratch read).

## Idea

Different students want different spaces. The film moves through four real
Tailor Homes apartments, each with its own character:

- **S. Eufemia:** orange wall, terrazzo, beams.
- **Via Nullo:** herringbone floors, grey walls, a study desk.
- **Vicolo Romano:** classic rooms, a yellow accent.
- **Colore & Design:** green curtains, a reading corner.

The photography is the hero. Graphics frame, sequence and caption it, then
land one clear offer: **15% off for UniPD and Erasmus students, verified via
WhatsApp.**

## Brand system (from the supplied “Studenti & Erasmus” creative)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Navy | `#1F2A48`, offer and claim scenes |
| Cream | `#F5F0E8`, opening and end card |
| Salmon (labels) | `#E9A28B`, tracked uppercase labels |
| Coral (offer pill) | `#C0533F`, the −15% pill, matching the photo creative's badge |
| Headlines | Cormorant Garamond Italic (Medium/SemiBold), sentence case, as in “15% di sconto per studenti UniPD ed Erasmus.” |
| Labels | Jost SemiBold, uppercase, tracking ≈ 0.28 em |
| Support | DM Sans Medium |
| Logo | Supplied Tailor Homes lockup (rasterised from `brand/01-noBgColor.svg`, unmodified), on cream only, as in the supplied logo image |

Graphic vocabulary is deliberately restrained: rectangular photo panels with
small radii, thin cream or salmon rules, one pill shape, and generous negative
space. No glow, particles, glass, gradients or bounce.

## Motion language

- **Photo motion:** slow push-ins (≤ 6%), gentle lateral drift and subtle
  parallax between panel and type.
- **Transitions:**
  - Panel expansions: a vertical photo panel grows into full frame.
  - Wall-edge wipes: the cut follows a real vertical in the photo (stair glass,
    door frame).
  - Split screens that slide in from opposite directions.
  - Editorial tiles that settle into a grid.
  - A mosaic that collapses into the end card.
- **Easing:** cubic and expo ease-in-out only. Type reveals word by word from
  a mask (line slides up).
- **Pacing:** visual changes land on the voice's phrase starts, measured from
  the WAV.

## Claims policy

- **On screen:** only the offer as worded in the supplied creative and brief,
  “15% off your booking for UniPD & Erasmus students; verify your student card
  via WhatsApp”, plus what is visible in the photos (furnished rooms, kitchen,
  study corner).
- **Not shown:** Wi-Fi, bills included, flexible stays, neighbourhoods or
  “centro storico”. They could not be verified because tailorhomes.it is blocked
  from this environment.
- **No partnership implied:** no university or Erasmus marks are reproduced.
  “UniPD / Erasmus” appears as plain text describing who qualifies. The supplied
  photo creative with the UniPD bag and Erasmus passport is not used, because it
  shows official marks.
- **WhatsApp:** written as text with a generic chat-bubble and check-mark
  glyph. The WhatsApp logo is not redrawn.

## Open questions and blockers

1. **Final voice-over.** `audio/voice/tailorhomes-students-vo.wav` has not been
   supplied. Timing uses a clearly labelled scratch read (AI TTS) and is
   re-derived automatically when the WAV is added.
2. **Student page verification.** tailorhomes.it is blocked by the environment's
   network policy, so the offer wording relies on the supplied creative. Please
   confirm any conditions (e.g. “per te, amici e famiglia”, booking channel) before
   publication.
3. **Music.** No licensed track was supplied. An original code-generated bed is
   used, as in Film 01, and documented.
